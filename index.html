Spreadsheets lose assets. Field-verified data doesn't

Most fixed asset registers drift from reality within a year — assets move floors, get transferred between departments, or quietly disappear. Here's what changes once Assetvisors is in the loop.

Without Assetvisors

  • No physical proof an asset exists where the books say it does
  • Ghost assets keep depreciating on paper for years

  • Photo-documented condition speeds up every claim
